How Marketplace works

Marketplace is a place to discover products offered by independent sellers. Product descriptions, photos, prices, stock, and seller information should be reviewed on the individual listing before placing an order.

Unless a listing clearly says otherwise, the seller—not Marketplace—provides and describes the product. Contact us if information is unclear or if you need help finding the right seller.

Shopping and orders

  • Review the listing, total, seller, and selected payment method before confirming an order.
  • Prices, availability, discounts, auction rules, and sale end times are shown on the relevant product listing and may change before an order is placed.
  • Keep your order confirmation and contact the seller or Marketplace with the order details if you need help.
  • Never share your password or one-time security codes with a seller or another shopper.

Delivery

Delivery options, costs, coverage, and timing can depend on the seller and the order. Check the listing or ask the seller to confirm these details before purchase. If an order is delayed or you cannot reach the seller, contact Marketplace with your order information.

Returns, cancellations, and refunds

Return eligibility and arrangements may vary by seller, item, and applicable law. Review the seller’s terms before ordering and contact the seller promptly if an item arrives damaged, is incorrect, or you want to request a return or cancellation. Marketplace can help direct your request, but this guide does not promise a particular return window or refund outcome.

Your rights under applicable consumer-protection laws are not replaced by this summary.

Privacy and account safety

Provide only accurate information needed for your account, order, or support request, and keep your sign-in details private. Do not include passwords or payment-card details in messages to sellers or support.

A complete privacy notice should explain what personal data is collected, why it is used, how long it is kept, who it is shared with, and how people can exercise their rights. Contact Marketplace with privacy questions at kamegnesandra@gmail.com.

Seller expectations

Sellers should provide truthful listings, accurate prices and stock information, clear delivery and return details, and timely responses to customers. Sellers are responsible for ensuring that their products and business practices comply with applicable law.

Marketplace may review seller or product content and take action under its terms. A published seller agreement should set out the specific review, commission, payout, dispute, and suspension rules.

Product details and responsible shopping

We encourage shoppers to ask about materials, origin, care, and product suitability when details are missing. Marketplace does not independently certify ethical sourcing, sustainability, or product claims unless a listing explicitly identifies verifiable evidence.
